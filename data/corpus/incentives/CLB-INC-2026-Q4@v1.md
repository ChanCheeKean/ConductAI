# 2026 Q4 incentive plan

## §2 Points

Points may be earned for balance transfers, credit-line increases, add-on enrollments, and Flex plans.

## §3.2 Saves

A product change on a close or annual-fee call counts as a save.

## §5 Evidence boundary

Incentive context is never evidence of individual misconduct.
