# Balance-transfer offers

## §OFR-BT-12-3 12-month offer

0% for 12 months with a 3% transfer fee.

## §OFR-BT-15-3 Promotional segment

0% for 15 months with a 3% fee; eligible promotional segment only.

## §OFR-BT-15-5 15-month offer

0% for 15 months with a 5% fee. Go-to APR equals the standard purchase APR.
