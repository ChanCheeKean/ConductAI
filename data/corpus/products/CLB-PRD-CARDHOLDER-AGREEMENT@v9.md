# Cardholder agreement

## §4.2 Late fee

A late fee may be charged up to $32.

## §4.3 Returned payment

The returned-payment fee is $29.

## §6.1 Annual-fee refund

Refund the annual fee in full when the account closes within 30 days of its posting.

## §7.1 Product changes

Convert rewards at the applicable product fact-sheet conversion rate.
