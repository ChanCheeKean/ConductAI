# CardShield fact sheet

## §2.1 Premium

$0.89 per $100 of ending statement balance each month.

## §2.2 When charged

Charge whenever ending balance exceeds zero, including when the customer later pays in full.

## §3 Cancellation

Cancel anytime; no premium after cancellation.
