# Regulation Z §1026.52 penalty-fee abridgement

## §1026.52(b) Penalty fees

Penalty fees must be reasonable and proportional. Regulation Z provides inflation-indexed safe-harbor amounts; this abridgement intentionally asserts no current dollar amount.
