# Servicemembers Civil Relief Act §3937 abridgement

## §3937(a) Six-percent maximum

Interest on qualifying obligations incurred before military service, including credit-card debt, is limited to six percent during the covered period; excess interest is forgiven, not deferred.

## §3937(b) Notice and orders

The servicemember supplies written notice and qualifying military orders or other appropriate evidence within the statutory period.
