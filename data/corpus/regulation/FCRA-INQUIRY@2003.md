# FCRA inquiry mechanics abridgement

## §604 Permissible purpose

A consumer report may be obtained only for a permissible purpose authorized by the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

## §INQ-1 Hard and soft inquiry mechanics

Industry terminology distinguishes inquiries visible to other creditors from account-review or promotional inquiries. Exact scoring effects vary; colleagues must not promise a precise score outcome. This mechanics summary is marked unverified.
