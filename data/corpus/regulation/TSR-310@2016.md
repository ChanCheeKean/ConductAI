# Telemarketing Sales Rule abridgement

## §310.3(a)(1) Material disclosures

Before a customer pays, a telemarketer must truthfully disclose required material information about the offered goods or services.

## §310.4(a)(7) Express informed consent

A telemarketer must obtain the consumer's express informed consent to be charged using the payment method.
