# 2024 late-fee amendment — vacated

## §1026.52(b)(1)(ii)(B) Vacated $8 safe harbor

The 2024 rule created an $8 late-fee safe harbor for larger issuers. A court vacated the rule on 2025-04-15; it is not governing policy and the prior framework applies.
