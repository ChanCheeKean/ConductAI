# Credit-line increase policy

## §2.1 Inquiry

Use a soft inquiry unless account tenure is under 12 months or the requested increase exceeds $5,000; either exception requires a hard inquiry.

## §2.2 Disclosure

Tell the customer before submission whenever a hard inquiry applies.

## §3.1 Ability to pay

Collect and evaluate income and obligations.
