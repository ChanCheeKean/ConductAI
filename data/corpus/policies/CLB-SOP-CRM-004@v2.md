# Fair conduct monitoring

## §2.1 Customer features

Do not use age, birth year, language, accent, national origin, ASR confidence as risk, or site.

## §2.2 Colleague features

Do not use demographics, site, or team membership as evidence against a colleague.

## §3.1 Pattern boundary

Never extend one colleague's pattern to teammates.
