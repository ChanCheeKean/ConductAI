# Hardship and vulnerability

## §3.1 Indicators

Indicators are confusion about caller or purpose; third-party reliance; inability to restate terms; repeated deferential assent; disclosed job loss or reduced income; bereavement or serious illness.

## §3.2 Two indicators

With two or more indicator types, pause any sale and offer a callback or trusted-contact involvement.

## §4.1 Active hardship

Do not sell Flex Installments, balance transfers, credit-line increases, or upgrades during an active Hardship Relief Plan.

## §5.1 Age excluded

Age, birth year, and assumed age are never vulnerability indicators.
