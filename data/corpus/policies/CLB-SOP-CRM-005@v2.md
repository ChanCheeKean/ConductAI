# Agent-memory governance

## §2 Lifecycle

Memory-note states are active, superseded, retracted, archived, and purged.

## §3.1 Supersede

When a governing source changes, supersede the note and set valid_to.

## §3.2 Retract

Retract a note that was wrong from its creation.

## §4.1 Colleague scope

Colleague notes must be evidence-backed, time-bounded, and scoped to one colleague; do not import them to teammates.

## §5.1 Purge

Purge a note relying on a prohibited basis and retain a tombstone.

## §6.1 Consolidate

Consolidate raw observations into one validity-bounded note with sources.
