# Sales consent and disclosure

## §3.1 Affirmative consent

Obtain a clear verbal yes to the specific named product after its price and material terms. Minimal assent to a statement or tag question is not consent.

## §3.2 Terms before consent

Give price and material terms before asking for consent.

## §4.1 Enrollment timing

Submit only during the interaction after consent.

## §5.3 Clear delivery

Required disclosures must be delivered clearly.

## §5.4 Questions

Answer price or term questions before enrollment.

## §6.1 No bundling

Never condition servicing or a fee waiver on a sale.
