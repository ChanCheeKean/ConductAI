# Conduct-review selection

## §3.1 Capacity

Apply weekly review capacity.

## §3.2 Random slice

Allocate 10% of capacity to a seeded, channel-stratified random slice using seed 20261116.

## §3.3 Permitted signals

Permitted signals: sale or enrollment, outbound sale, cancellation within 30 days, complaint within seven days, recording gap, protected-situation flag, scanner flag, and prior substantiated findings in 90 days.

## §3.4 Prohibited selection features

Never select using customer age, language, accent, ASR confidence, site, or colleague demographics.

## §4.1 Decision SLA

Decide within 10 business days after the later of interaction date and selection or trigger date. Exclude weekends and US bank holidays.
