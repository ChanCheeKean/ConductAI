# Complaint handling

## §2.1 Definition

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, fee, or colleague, whether or not the customer says “complaint.”

## §3.1 Logging

Log the complaint in the same interaction with received_at equal to the expression time and disposition COMPLAINT.

## §3.2 Acknowledgment

Acknowledge within five business days.

## §3.3 Underlying validity

A valid underlying fee does not remove the duty to log dissatisfaction.
