# SCRA referral

## §2.1 Trigger

Any mention of military orders, active duty, mobilization, or deployment triggers the process.

## §3.1 Open review

Open an SCRA benefits review in the same interaction.

## §3.2 Explain scope

Explain that the six-percent cap covers qualifying pre-service obligations, including credit cards.

## §4.1 Eligibility

The SCRA benefits team decides eligibility; the servicing colleague does not.
