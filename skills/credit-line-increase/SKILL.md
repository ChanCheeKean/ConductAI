# Review CLI inquiry disclosure and ability to pay

1. Resolve policy by interaction date.
2. Compute tenure and requested increase thresholds.
3. Distinguish an authorized inquiry from misinformation about its effect.

Stop when the exact assurance, governing policy version, request facts, and actual inquiry type agree. Do not generalize a case-local assurance into durable memory.
